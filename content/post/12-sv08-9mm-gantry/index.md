## Overview
I redesigned the Sovol SV08 gantry to support 9mm GT2 belts instead of the stock 6mm setup.
## The upgrade
- redesigned gantry parts
- double shear motor mounts with bearing support
- wider pulleys and idlers
- longer shaft stepper motors
