## Overview
ABS & ASA warping - ruining your prints?
I insulated my Sovol SV08 enclosure to see if better heat retention alone could fix it — no firmware, no electronics, just insulation and physics.
## Results
📈 +10°C / ~25% higher chamber temperature\
🔥 Fewer warped ABS prints\
🧪 Simple, repeatable experiment anyone can try\
\
If you’re printing ABS, ASA, or high-temp filaments, this might be the easiest enclosure mod you’ll ever do.
