## Overview
I was struggling to film clean top down shots while working on my SV08 gantry upgrade project…
So I built a simple overhead camera rig from DN40 pipes, a few 3D printed parts, and some basic hardware.

I’m currently filming all my videos with my iPhone, and this rig completely improved the way I record overhead shots for maker and workshop content.
## 3D printed parts
Feet: [Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/1731347-dn40-pipes-camera-rig-feet)  
## BOM
- 2x 87° pieces
- 2x DN40 pipe 500mm
- 3x DN40 pipe 1000mm
