## Overview

My girlfriend needed a PC for Minecraft, Palworld and Hogwarts Legacy, so instead of buying a new system I decided to build one as cheaply as possible using a mix of old hardware and used parts.

The final cost: just **€150**.

To make things even more interesting, I'm running **Bazzite Linux** instead of Windows. In this video I build the PC, install Linux and Steam, and then find out how well this extremely cheap gaming PC actually performs.
___
## Tested Games
Everything tested on 1080p.
You can find more informations in the video.
* **Minecraft** - Fancy Setting
* **Palworld** - Medium Setting
* **Hogwarts Legacy** - Medium Setting except Shadows on low
___
## Hardware
* **CPU**: Intel Core i7 4770
* **RAM**: 16GB from Kingston
* **Case**: Sharkoon Midi Tower
* **Motherboard**: Gigabyte GA-H87-D3H
* **SSD**: 240GB from Kingston
* **Power Supply**: BeQuit 600W
* **CPU Cooler**: thermaltake Contac 9 SE
