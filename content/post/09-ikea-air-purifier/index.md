## Overview
I upgraded the IKEA UPPÅTVIND air purifier to make it more useful for 3D printing. The stock filter works well for particles, but it doesn't do much for VOCs and odors.\
So I designed a 3D printable add-on that adds an activated carbon stage — while keeping airflow and using the original filter.
