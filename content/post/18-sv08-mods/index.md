## Overview
I've been using the Sovol SV08 for over two years now, and during that time I've made quite a few modifications to improve its reliability, performance, and overall usability.

In this post, I'll go through all the mods I'm currently using on my SV08 — from essential fixes I'd recommend to almost every owner, to quality-of-life improvements and more specialized upgrades for specific use cases.

For each mod, you'll find a short explanation along with links to the required 3D printed parts, guides, and related videos where available.

---
## Essential Mods
### Eddy Current Probe
Replaces the stock inductive probe for faster bed meshing and more reliable Z-offset calibration.
- [Printables - BTT Eddy Mount Stock Toolhead](https://www.printables.com/model/968900-sovol-sv08-btt-eddy-mount)  
- [BTT Eddy](https://biqu.equipment/products/bigtreetech-eddy)  
- [Cartographer3D](https://cartographer3d.com/)  
- [YouTube-Video](https://youtu.be/BEIvhQsFwNQ)  
### Taco Bed Fix
Uses rubber spacers under the bed to allow each mounting bolt to be tightened individually, helping to reduce bed deformation and achieve a flatter print surface.
- [GitHub](https://github.com/Wrath669/SV08BedTension/blob/main/README.md)  
- [YouTube-Video](https://youtu.be/xGcPHl1sPP0)  

### Gantry Fix
Replaces the stock XY joints with corrected parts to improve belt alignment and dimensional accuracy.
- [Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/1154812-sovol-sv08-side-slide-carriage-make-parallel-to-th)  
- [YouTube-Video](https://youtu.be/TQyLP3SBOJE)

## Recommended Mods
### 9mm Belt Conversion
Upgrades the motion system from 6mm to 9mm belts for higher belt tension, improved rigidity, and better performance at high speeds.
- [Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/1734135-sv08-9mm-gantry-upgrade-double-shear-support)  
- [Youtube-Video](https://youtu.be/P4obmA0hvgc)  
### Cable Chain
Keeps the toolhead and stepper cables properly guided and prevents them from getting caught while the printer is moving.
- [Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/1008587-sovol-sv08-cable-chain)  
### Bottom-Mounted Filament Spool
Moves the filament spool to the bottom of the printer to lower its center of gravity and reduce vibrations.
- [Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/928589-sovol-sv08-side-mount-spool-holder-remix)  
### Electronics Cooling
Adds dedicated fans for the MOSFET and controller board to improve cooling inside the electronics bay.
- [Printables MOSFET](https://www.printables.com/model/1268452-sovol-sv08-mosfet-fan-bracket)  
- [Printables Controller Board](https://www.printables.com/model/1104389-sovol-sv08-elecronics-8010-fan-mount)  
### HULA Feet
Uses horizontally moving bearings to isolate printer vibrations and reduce the amount of movement transferred to the surface below.
- [Printables HULA](https://www.printables.com/model/898357-hula-v10-for-voron-printers)  
- [Printables SV08 Feet](https://www.printables.com/model/923491-hula-sovol-sv08)

## Specific Mods
### Enclosure
Turns the open-frame SV08 into an enclosed printer for more stable chamber temperatures when printing materials like ABS or ASA.
- [Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/879399-sovol-sv08-panels-for-the-enclosure)  
### Top Hat
Adds extra clearance above the enclosure so the toolhead cable can move freely when printing near the maximum Z height.
- [Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/865851-sovol-sv08-top-hat)  
### Enclosure Insulation
Adds insulation to the enclosure panels to reduce heat loss and achieve higher chamber temperatures.
- [YouTube-Video](https://youtu.be/ZUGgIxuJ_KQ)  
- [Insulation](https://s.click.aliexpress.com/e/_c2u8JP61)  

### 270° Door Hinges
Allows the enclosure doors to fold completely around the sides of the printer, keeping them out of the way when opened.
- [Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/1531474-sv08-270deg-hinges-cn3d-enclosure)  

### Filter Modules
Uses blower fans and activated carbon to continuously filter enclosure air and help reduce odors and VOCs while printing.
- [Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/1216287-sovol-sv08-filter)  
### Toolhead
Replaces the stock toolhead with alternative setups such as the Dragon Burner or A4T for greater flexibility in hotend, extruder, and toolhead-board configurations.
- [Dragonburner](https://github.com/chirpy2605/voron/tree/main/V0/Dragon_Burner)  
- [A4T](https://github.com/Armchair-Heavy-Industries/A4T)  
- [Nitehawk36 USB Mount](https://www.printables.com/model/1297243-nitehawk-36-usb-adapter-2020-mount)
