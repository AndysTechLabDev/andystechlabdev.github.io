## Overview
I was tired of filament rolls piling up everywhere, so I turned two IKEA VESKEN shelves into a massive 42-spool filament storage rack.

With a simple 3D printed spacer upgrade from Printables, the setup becomes a super cheap alternative to expensive filament shelves like the BIQU Panda storage system.
