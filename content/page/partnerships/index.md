## Let's build something together

I'm always open to meaningful collaborations with brands, companies and agencies whose products or services are a good fit for my audience.

Andy's Tech Lab focuses on practical technology, DIY projects, 3D printing, homelab setups, open-source tools and useful workflows. I aim to create content that is honest, relevant and genuinely helpful to readers and viewers.

---

## What I offer

Depending on the project, collaborations may include:

- sponsored blog posts
- product reviews
- product integrations
- affiliate partnerships
- long-term brand collaborations
- other custom campaign formats

Every partnership is discussed individually so that the format matches both the product and the campaign goals.

---

## Editorial Standards

Honesty and transparency are fundamental values of Andy's Tech Lab.

Any sponsored collaboration will always be clearly disclosed, and all opinions expressed are entirely my own. A partnership does not influence my review, conclusions or recommendations.

I am committed to providing honest, unbiased and constructive feedback, highlighting both strengths and potential weaknesses whenever appropriate. This ensures that my audience can trust the information I share and make informed decisions.

Receiving a product, service or financial compensation does not guarantee positive coverage, publication or a recommendation. Every product is evaluated independently based on my real-world experience and whether it provides value to my audience.

My audience's trust is more important than any sponsorship. Therefore, every review reflects my genuine experience and honest opinion, including both positive and negative aspects whenever relevant.
